## Strategic call
1,236 followers at 125 median reach will never be a lead channel. **Stop treating LASSO IG as lead gen.** Its job is credibility — a gym owner checks the grid before replying to an ad, podcast, or DM. Today that check returns 66 text cards, no faces, and the same caption 4x.

Goal: the grid **closes** traffic other channels send it, and the Summit-style ask repeats weekly instead of once a quarter.
