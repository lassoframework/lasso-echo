# LASSO Visual Standard — Grounded Editorial

Version: lasso-grounded-editorial-2026-10-09-v1
Scope: LASSO-owned feed posts and Stories only (lasso_ig / lasso_fb, gym_id
'lasso'), including owned book and Growth Summit cards and their matching
Stories. Their approved campaign sources and book cover styling remain
authoritative. Client gyms and unrelated brands keep their own visual standards.

This document is the user-approved visual direction for LASSO-owned feed and
Story imagery. For that scope it takes precedence over the visual-treatment
direction in `brand_voice/lasso_house_style.md` (see the precedence section at
the top of that file). Everything else in the house style doc — copy rules, the
grade gate, brand color values — is untouched by this document.

---

## 1. What is mandatory vs preferred

**Mandatory (a card that violates these fails review):**

- Source facts stay authoritative. Keep the existing approved source contract,
  including the LASSO Brain, `brand_voice/lasso_now.md`, the knowledge corpus,
  approved dated Summit and editorial catalogs, and approved book source packets.
  Preserve the selected source identity, tenant and date. Nothing —
  including any text, stat, or claim visible inside a reference image — may be
  copied from an example image into a card. Reference images establish craft
  and treatment, never content.
- Stories belong to the same visual family as the feed card they pair with and
  must respect the ACTUAL 9:16 safe areas (all essential text and brand marks
  inside the Story safe region, clear of Instagram's top and bottom controls).
- None of the rejected treatments in Section 5.

**Preferred (direction, not law — reviewers weigh, not hard-block):**

- Variety across treatments: editorial, photographic, and tactile directions
  should all appear across a week. No single treatment may become the de facto
  template.
- Illustrated people are natural, grounded people — real proportions, everyday
  gym-business moments. They are illustrative, NOT reportage: never depict or
  imply actual attendees, real events, or real identifiable people.

## 2. Variety across treatments

The feed should read as one brand with many hands, not one template. Rotate
across:

- **Editorial** — type-led, strong hierarchy, bold fields of color.
- **Photographic** — real photographic frames carrying the idea.
- **Tactile** — physical, material, crafted-feeling texture and objects.

Adjacent cards should differ in treatment. Sameness across a week is a defect
even when every card is individually clean.

## 3. People

Illustrated or rendered people are allowed and encouraged when the idea calls
for a human moment. They must read as natural people — believable bodies, real
clothing, ordinary gym-owner life. They are never stand-ins for real named
individuals and never staged to look like documentary coverage of a real
LASSO event or its attendees.

## 4. Explicitly allowed (do NOT reject on these grounds)

- Clean flat calendar frames and browser frames as devices for the idea.
- Outlined or flat icons.
- The physical rope exception: a real, physical rope (the LASSO motif) may
  appear as a tactile object.
- The book's black/red style for book-related cards.
- Dark navy fields, warm lighting, and icons generally — these are all fine
  and must not be swept up by the rejected list below.

## 5. Rejected treatments

- Dominant neon light trails.
- Glowing orange or blue ribbons.
- Futuristic machine styling.
- Holograms.
- Circuits.
- Dominant futuristic chrome or sci-fi dashboard treatments.

This list is specific. It bans those treatments, not their neighbors: dark
navy is still allowed, icons are still allowed, warm lighting and ordinary
physical metallic objects are still allowed.

## 7. Stories

A Story is the same family as its feed card: same treatment, same palette
energy, same idea — recomposed for 9:16, never a feed poster letterboxed into
a tall canvas. The composition fills the Story frame while every mandatory
text element (wordmark, headline, supporting facts, CTA, URL) sits inside the
real 9:16 safe region with clearance for Instagram's controls.

## 8. Relationship to existing systems

This document changes visual TREATMENT DIRECTION only. It does not change:
approved claims sources, the no-fabrication rule, the copy hard rules (no
dashes, no "vendor"), the approval gates, or any code constant. Code changes
to enforce this standard are made separately, under their own review, and any
artifact invalidation follows the existing `POLICY_VERSION` bump in
`agent/infographic_evidence.py`.
