While your audit is in the works, one idea that will change how you read it:

Reach tells you how many people saw you. Conversion tells you whether any of it mattered. A feed with 50k views and a dead bio link earns less than a feed with 900 views and a working path to join.

So before anyone tells you to post more or spend more, check the legs in this order:

▸Close rate — of the people who show up, do 70%+ join?
▸Show rate — of the people who book, do 50%+ walk in?
▸Booking behavior — of the leads, do 50%+ book?
▸Lead volume — only now does “more eyeballs” matter

Fixing a broken leg upstream doubles everything downstream — for free. That's why your report card grades the path, not the follower count.
